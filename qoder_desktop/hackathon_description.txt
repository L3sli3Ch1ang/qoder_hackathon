Alibaba Cloud x Qoder Hackathon Singapore 2026

Onsite Day (22 July)
    ​Opening & Product Intro: A brief Qoder product introduction from the Alibaba Cloud / Qoder team. Because the tool has a very low barrier to entry, this is designed to get every attendee — including non-developers — hands-on quickly.
    ​Hands-on Build Time: Start building your Qoder project on-site with mentor support from Qoder engineers.
    ​No On-site Judging: Projects don't need to be finalized on the day. You'll leave with a clear submission framework and continue building post-event.

​Post-Event (2 Weeks)
    ​Finalize your project at your own pace.
    ​Post your project on LinkedIn or X and tag the official Alibaba Cloud and Qoder accounts.
    ​Include a short write-up and/or demo video alongside your social post.

​Judging Criteria
    ​Innovation & Creativity — 25% — Originality of idea; creative use of Qoder's Quest Mode + Expert Mode
    ​Use of Qoder — 30% — Effective use of agentic capabilities, AI integration, workflow reusability
    ​Impact — 20% — Solves a real-world problem; likelihood of real-world adoption
    ​Technical Execution — 15% — Completeness (incl. demo video), end-to-end functionality, appropriate complexity
    ​Presentation & UGC — 10% — Clear explanation in videos, social post, engagement, correct tagging

Tips for a Winning Build 
1 Start with a Spec 
	Write a clear spec before jumping into Quest Mode. The better your spec, the better Qoder's output. 
2 Scope Small, Ship Big 
	Pick a focused problem. 
	A polished small project beats an unfinished ambitious one. 
3 Use Quest Mode Early 
	Let Qoder handle the scaffolding and boilerplate. Spend your time on the creative parts. 
4 Record as You 
	Go Capture screenshots and notes during your build — they'll make your demo video and social post easier. 
5 Ask for Help 
	Mentors are here for you! Don't spin your wheels — ask early, ask often.
